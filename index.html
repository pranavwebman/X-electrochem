Daniell Cell (Zn | ZnSO4 || CuSO4 | Cu)

Electrical neutrality maintained
Operating Normally: Zinc has a stronger oxidation tendency than copper, so electrons travel from Zn (anode, −) through the wire to Cu (cathode, +). The KCl salt bridge maintains electrical neutrality without mixing the solutions.
Anode Reaction (Oxidation)
Zn(s) → Zn²⁺(aq) + 2e⁻
Cathode Reaction (Reduction)
Cu²⁺(aq) + 2e⁻ → Cu(s)
Overall Cell Reaction
Zn(s) + Cu²⁺(aq) → Zn²⁺(aq) + Cu(s)

Electrolysis of Acidified Water

DC Circuit Closed (6.0 V)
Acidified Water: Dilute H2SO4 increases water ionisation so current flows. Hydrogen gas (2 parts) collects at the negative cathode and oxygen gas (1 part) collects at the positive anode.
Anode (+) — Oxidation
2H2O → O2(g) + 4H⁺ + 4e⁻
Cathode (−) — Reduction
4H⁺ + 4e⁻ → 2H2(g)
Key Product / Observation
H2 : O2 = 2 : 1 volume ratio
